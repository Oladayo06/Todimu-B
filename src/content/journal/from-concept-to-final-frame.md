A strong image matters. So does the idea behind it, the people at its centre and the decisions that carry it from script to screen.

## Story before spectacle

Every creative decision should serve the story. I begin by understanding the subject, the audience and the emotional core, then build the visual approach around what the work needs to communicate.

## One process, not four departments

I work across development, writing, producing, directing, cinematography and editing. That connected perspective helps keep the original intention alive through every stage of production — from the first treatment to the final delivery file.

> Strong visuals and polished post-production should do more than impress. They should make the work clearer, more memorable and more meaningful to the people watching.
