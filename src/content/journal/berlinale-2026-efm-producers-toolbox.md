I’ve been selected as one of 35 global producers for the **Berlinale 2026 European Film Market Producer’s Toolbox** programme in Germany.

The programme sits alongside the European Film Market at the Berlin International Film Festival and focuses on film and documentary production.

## Why it matters to the work

My work brings development, production and post-production into one creative process. Programmes like this sharpen the producing side of that process: how a story is developed, packaged and carried through to the people who will watch it.
